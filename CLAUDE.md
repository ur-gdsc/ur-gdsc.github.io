# Agent Instructions

This site is the public GDSC University of Rochester website. Preserve the public story and club history when making changes.

## Content That Must Stay

- Keep the `Featured Speakers` section and preserve existing featured speakers unless the user explicitly asks to remove or replace specific speakers. New speakers may be added to `SPEAKERS` or `UPCOMING_SPEAKERS`; additions should not delete or overwrite the existing roster.
- Keep the `See What We're Building` highlights section and preserve its existing cards unless the user explicitly asks to remove or replace specific items. New highlights, recaps, photos, workshops, socials, and project snapshots may be added to this section; additions should extend the section rather than overwrite it.
- Keep `Leadership Archive` on the page. Past leaders should remain visible as archived leadership, not deleted.

## Leadership Rules

- Current leaders belong in a current `Leadership` section.
- Past leaders belong in `Leadership Archive`.
- The 2025-2026 eboard is now past leadership and should stay in `Leadership Archive`.
- When a future user says the academic year ended, the current `Leadership` roster should be moved into `Leadership Archive`, with its academic year label preserved.
- When adding a new eboard, add it as current `Leadership` above the archive. Do not overwrite or delete the archived eboards.

## Implementation Notes

- Avoid broad visual rewrites unless requested. Keep the existing dark, restrained GDSC visual style.
