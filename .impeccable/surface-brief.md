# Homepage

## Direction contract

THESIS: A recognizable Rochester student community, built around real people and events. Preserve the compactness the user liked while removing the literal social-network costume they found odd.

OWN-WORLD: Restrained navy canvas, pale blue accents, self-hosted Archivo display and Source Sans 3 body, lighter white headings and brighter body text, quiet rules, real chapter photos and recognizable GDSC branding. Four-pixel control corners. No Facebook wordmark, sidebar, or simulated social features.

STORY: See the people and activity, browse complete event recaps, discover speakers and current leaders, then join or find an event. Preserve all content and the full leadership archive.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Centered 1160px single-page composition. Compact navigation. Original two-line slogan and chapter identity on the left; large borderless existing NASA event group photo on the right. Join and event links directly below the intro. Photo gallery begins below, followed by speakers, current and archived leadership, events, and mission.

FORM: Refinement of the prior experiment according to the user's latest feedback. Direct implementation using existing chapter assets. No generated comp. Recaps use native details; all other navigation is native anchors.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
