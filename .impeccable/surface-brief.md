OWN-WORLD: User rejected oversized editorial typography and Archivo as generic. Material-style rounded surfaces and pill actions follow the user's Google-brand preference.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full club logo lockup at left in compact navigation. Two-column opening with the original motto and join action on the left, existing NASA Stories photograph and caption on the right. Mobile stacks at smaller scale. Highlights retain an asymmetric event-photo spread; remaining sections use compact directories and portrait layouts.

FORM: Brand-led refinement following explicit user correction. The prior printed-annual direction (seed 1098214770) is superseded: replace its masthead, 96px slogan, giant footer, and Archivo with Google-brand typography, smaller scale, Material shapes, and fuller identity. No generated imagery.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
