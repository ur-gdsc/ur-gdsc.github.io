# Product

## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

The public Google Developer Student Club website at the University of Rochester presents the chapter, speakers, events, projects, and leadership history.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Preserve every existing featured speaker and highlight, the current leadership roster, and the Leadership Archive. The 2025–2026 board is archived. The current board is 2026–2027. Speaker search, archive filters, event links, Instagram, and contact links remain functional.

## Brand Commitments

Keep the GDSC identity and club history. The user requested a cooler visual direction with some early Facebook aesthetic after rejecting the first polish pass. The user subsequently asked to improve the experiment after it was perceived as odd; keep its useful compactness and make the club identity and real community more prominent. Retain the existing restrained dark base.

## Evidence on Hand

AGENTS.md, constants.ts, public chapter imagery, and the existing component copy. Do not invent events, membership counts, activity, or social features.

## Content accuracy

Public biographical and company claims were reviewed October 27, 2026; see FACT_CHECK.md for evidence, corrections, and verification limits. Keep source links with biographies and recaps. Date and attribute funding/user/partner milestones; distinguish later company updates from event-time context. Do not promise cloud credits, weekly schedules, specific-company mentorship, or current competition eligibility without evidence. The 2026–2027 board remains chapter-supplied; older Google organizer profiles do not supersede it. Private-event claims require chapter confirmation.
